The following is not financial advise:
This indicator works best on the daily charts. There is more information displayed than appears.
Firstly the inputs menu in settings has three profit targets for three 4th dimension scopes each applied to a different magnitude of market change with in the vortex. Magnitude one controls the high frequency trigger, it is represented by the small red or green dot. Magnitude ten controls the core (medium) frequency trigger, it is represented by the larger red or green dot. Magnitude 100 represents the low frequency trigger it is represented by the large red or green dot. The small orange dot represents continuation on the high frequency.
The low frequency colour shows green for bull dominance and red for bear dominance. But with additional effect that when the low frequency vortex closes a strong top or bottom signal is present.
The core frequency shows local market sentiment, bull/bear, but as the other frequency waves cross through the core frequency it can be used to scale entries, exits and tend strength. 
The high frequency colour show favorable entry and exits, local tops local bottoms.
The three signals combined form stronger signals.
  These signals not for failing assets:
When the low frequency signal shows red the bottom is in or near, make a long term investment soon or abandon a failed asset. With a asset proven to recover like bitcoin purchase volume should increase.
When the core frequency signal shows red then bear trend is continuing. 
When the high frequency signal is red the best short term buying opportunity is available. Accumulation, Dollar Cost Average, day trading etc.
When the core and high frequency signal is red this shows good investment opportunity, increase investment amount. 
If all three signals are present as red on a single bar you can read that at the bottom is in or near, a large buy will pay off in the long term, a medium buy soon will pay off in the medium term, and you should enter the market on this bar the next bar or three bars time.
Volume will confirm most signals.
The green signals call tops, how ever one can still buy into a bull run.
For every high frequency red dot you buy, a high frequency green dot will come to sell at the set profit target. Same with core and low.
I have set the default targets to 1.2 high frequency mag one vortex, 3 core mag ten vortex and 36 low mag one hundred vortex. 1+2 = 3, 3 + 3 = 6, 3 + 6 =9. Unity's law applies to greed, if you expect too much back the signal will never arrive.
